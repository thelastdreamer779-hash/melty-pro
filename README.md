# DELTAHOLE (working title)

A Deltarune × Black Hole Fishing mashup for [Melty](https://melty.gg). Kris and a friend explore a new Dark World where
Ted Herring has dropped black holes into the ponds, reel his fish out as Deltarune battles, and spend the
catch in his shop.

Status: design in progress. See `MODLOG.md` and `sheets/`.

Requires the player's own Deltarune and Black Hole Fishing; no game files are included.
